# Miniature Look Icon

Selected concept: a miniature outfit comprising a sage V-neck blouse, lilac wide-leg trousers and an ochre shoulder bag on warm ivory.

Created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, then exported with Lanczos resizing to RGB PNG at 32, 180, 192, 512 and 1024 pixels. The character artwork inside the app was not edited. Icon colors do not modify the app color library.

Final image-edit prompt: Preserve the selected miniature outfit artwork and its garment shapes, folds, textile texture and colors. Center the sage green V-neck blouse, lilac wide-leg trousers and ochre shoulder bag on a completely opaque, full-bleed warm ivory background (#F6EFE6). Keep the complete outfit comfortably inside the square canvas. No text, person, mannequin, hanger, border, rounded corners or additional objects. The operating system supplies the icon corners.

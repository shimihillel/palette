# Data and color-science references

## A Dictionary of Color Combinations, Volume 1

Book: Sanzo Wada; Seigensha, ISBN 978-4-86152-247-5.
Publisher: https://en.seigensha.com/books/978-4-86152-247-5/

The embedded digital reference set contains 159 sRGB references and 348 combination identifiers.
Data: https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations
Originally digitized by Dain M. Blodorn Kim; corrected/converted by Matt DesLauriers.
Retrieved 2026-09-26. Source colors.json SHA-256 recorded below.

These reference colors are NOT added to the app's selectable color library.
Outfits use the existing Shimi Looks colors, approximating the references under the app's role restrictions.
Two- and three-color plates are expanded into four distinct existing shades by repeating source color directions.
Some plates cannot meet the app's restrictions and are skipped. Digital sRGB values can differ from printed swatches.

# Shimi Looks illustration

## Version 4.1.4

The full-body illustration is the approved face, hairstyle and head-to-body connection preview, `assets/shimi-personal-v414.png`, at 1024 × 1536 pixels. The face and hairstyle follow the user-selected photograph. The head was slightly reduced and its connection to the neck and shoulders refined in the preview the user approved. The approved preview is copied byte-for-byte without another generation, rescaling, retouching or recompression.

The illustration wears a plain short-sleeve V-neck top, structured high-rise wide-leg trousers and loafers. The trousers have a flat waistband, button, belt loops, curved front pockets and wide straight legs. Cloth and denim use this same approved cut. The three garments are recolored through registered `art-matte-*-v414.png` layers that encode the source fabric RGB and fractional coverage. The face, neckline skin, small necklaces and tattoos are protected from recoloring. A subtle `denim-weave-v414.png` texture is confined to the trousers when denim is selected.

`art-background-v414.png` describes the source's external white backdrop. The display renderer blends this backdrop into the existing cream interface (#F7F3EC), leaving the source file intact. Main preview, enlargement and download use the same crop: x=204, y=0, width=536, height=1536. Only empty margins are cropped; the character is never distorted.

Development references, original photographs and rejected illustration variants are not included in this release package.

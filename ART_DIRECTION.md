The approved preview is copied byte-for-byte without another generation, rescaling, retouching or recompression.

Cloth and denim use this same approved cut and pose. The three garments are recolored through registered `art-matte-*-v415.png` layers that encode source fabric RGB and fractional coverage. Face, hair and skin are protected from recoloring. A subtle `denim-weave-v415.png` texture is confined to the trousers when denim is selected.

`art-background-v415.png` describes the source's external ivory backdrop. The renderer blends it into the existing cream interface (#F7F3EC), leaving the source file intact. Main preview, enlargement and download use the same crop: x=200, y=0, width=550, height=1659. Only empty margins are cropped; the character is never distorted.
